# Convexity Desk: Privacy & Positioning Taglines

This document serves as a repository for marketing taglines and philosophical positioning statements regarding Convexity Desk's unique value proposition: **100% local, air-gapped, institutional-grade risk management.**

Unlike platforms such as Kinfo or Tradervue, Convexity Desk does not require users to link their brokerage accounts via Plaid or exposing API keys. It respects the privacy of the trader's PnL and strategy.

## Core Differentiators (For Social Media / Marketing)

* **The Air-Gapped Advantage:** 100% local operation. Your PnL is your business. We don't ask for API keys, and we don't store your alpha on the cloud.
* **Active Quant Engine vs. Passive Diary:** We enforce discipline before you trade (Pre-Flight Matrix) rather than just telling you what you did wrong after you lost money.
* **Options 3D Topography:** While competitors just log option entry/exit prices, we map Gamma cliffs and Theta glide paths on an interactive Volatility Surface.
* **Market Flow Matrix:** Track institutional capital rotation and macro heat maps in real-time, giving you a forward-looking edge instead of just backward-looking reporting.
